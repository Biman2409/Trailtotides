# Trail, GPX and trek-facts audit (2026-11-26)

What was checked: all 55 built trails and the GPX files made from them (`trail.gpx`), the trek
page facts synced from them (distance, days, altitude, pin), every trail against the GPS tracks
people have uploaded to OpenStreetMap (`research/trail-traces.md`), the trail quality and
consistency checks (`research/trail-quality.md`, `research/trail-consistency.md`), the catalogue
audit and the booking links. The trail and GPX check is `scripts/research/audit-trail-data.ts`.

The GPX side is clean: every trail's points have coordinates and heights, every stop has a name
and height, side trips and detours are well formed, and stretches with no tracking data are named
in the file and marked with waypoints.

## Doodhpathri and Gulabi Kantha settled; final audit (2026-11-26, third pass)

**Doodhpathri, Gurwansar to Pathri ki Gali.** Operators (Trek The Himalayas) walk it on day 5,
"Gurwansar to Duriem via Pamsar Lake", 9 km. No one has recorded it: none of the 288 Wikiloc tracks
starting anywhere in 33.55–34.15 N, 74.15–74.85 E comes within 1.5 km of both Gurwansar and Pathri
ki Gali, and no OSM trace walks it. OSM showed the line had the place wrong: the point the
Greater Lakes track turns back at is Pathri ki Gali (a pass), and Pam Sar is a lake 900 m south-east
of it, with an unnamed pass north of Gurwansar. The line now goes Gurwansar → pass ("Pass above
Gurwansar", 4,196 m, the trek's high point) → Pam Sar → Pathri ki Gali, through those mapped points,
2.6 km marked as having no tracking data (`mapOnly` in trek-configs keeps map-placed points from
counting as tracked). Also moved, to where trekkers' tracks start and finish: Doodhpathri (the road
end, where four Wikiloc tracks start) and Tosa Maidan (where the Greater Lakes track finishes).
Doodhpathri → Gurwansar follows the Gurwansar Lakes track. 31.5 km; days 6.9, 5.7, 8.9 and 10 km
(operators: 6.5+4.5, 7, 9, 10-11+11).

**Gulabi Kantha summit.** Drawn, as a side trip from Seema Thatch along a Wikiloc track that walks
it (Kanasar to Bainkula, Mridul 2015, 13063359): 2.1 km each way and 590 m up to 3,836 m (3,852 m
on the GPS), against operators' "6 km there and back, 1,800 ft up, steep summit climb". Operators
quote 4,000-4,023 m; no ground within 3 km of camp reaches that, so the page now gives 3,836 m.
The "not drawn" note is gone. Side trips can now follow guide points (`guide` on a side entry).

**Audit results**
- Trail data and GPX (audit-trail-data.ts, now aware of side trips and duration buckets): 1 finding,
  Bajre Dara has no trail (no GPS track exists; its page says so).
- Trail quality: 0 to fix. Left as checks: Panch Kedar (its road stretches), Doodhpathri's 2.6 km
  with no tracking data, straights that match people's tracks, and days that climb 1,500 m+.
- Consistency: only Panch Kedar, whose check counts its drives as walking.
- Untracked across all 55 trails: 2.6 km, all on Doodhpathri.
- OSM traces (trail-traces.md): the same anonymous-point "off track" readings; Wikiloc tracks
  confirm the lines (see second pass).
- Catalogue: lead prices above base fees (by design), shared pins on shared summits, Extreme for Pin
