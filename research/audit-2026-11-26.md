# Trail, GPX and trek-facts audit (2026-11-26)

What was checked: all 55 built trails and the GPX files made from them (`trail.gpx`), the trek
page facts synced from them (distance, days, altitude, pin), every trail against the GPS tracks
people have uploaded to OpenStreetMap (`research/trail-traces.md`), the trail quality and
consistency checks (`research/trail-quality.md`, `research/trail-consistency.md`), the catalogue
audit and the booking links. The trail and GPX check is `scripts/research/audit-trail-data.ts`.

The GPX side is clean: every trail's points have coordinates and heights, every stop has a name
and height, side trips and detours are well formed, and stretches with no tracking data are named
in the file and marked with waypoints.

  Bikat on EBC via Gokyo).
- Booking links: 41 of 87 answered OK. The other 46 are all Trek The Himalayas pages that turned
  the check away for sending too many requests (429), so they aren't confirmed broken; recheck
  slowly.

## Not problems (checked and explained)

- Page altitudes above the line's high point on Valley of Flowers, Khopra Danda, Gokyo Lakes,
  Everest Base Camp, Brahmatal and Madhmaheshwar: the top is a side trip (Hemkund, Khayer Lake,
  Gokyo Ri, Kala Patthar, Brahmatal Top, Budha Madhmaheshwar).
- Deoriatal Chandrashila's days add to 28.3 km on a 22.4 km line: the Chandrashila day is out and
  back.
- Panch Kedar's day-km warnings: the circuit's days include drives, which the check counts as walking.
- Lead prices above the lowest operator fee: by design, the lead price is the full trip price.
