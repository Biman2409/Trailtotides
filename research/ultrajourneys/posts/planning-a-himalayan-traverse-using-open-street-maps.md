Planning a Himalayan Traverse using Open Street Maps

 June 29, 2021

 Peter Van Geit

In this post we are using Open Street Maps and Waymarked Hiking Trails to plan a traverse across 18 passes in Lahaul-Zanskar-Ladakh-Spiti-Kullu. Click on the links below to review maps for the individual sections and elevation profiles. For each section a blog post on ultrajourneys.org provides additional details.
