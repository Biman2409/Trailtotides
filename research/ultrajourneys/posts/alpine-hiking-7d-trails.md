Alpine Hiking 7D – Trails

 December 26, 2021

 Peter Van Geit

In the previous module we mapped human settlements based on an external geo-referenced data set. In this module we will map trails based on GPS recordings. Mapping trails (Lines) cannot be done through OSMAnd which only supports mapping of Points. We will use the standard OSM web client &#8220;osm.org&#8221; to map trails.

A friend of mine recently hiked the Kokand and Bail Ghat trail in the Sahyadri:

https://drive.google.com/file/d/19Y8qZNDPbyXAmmVeAqGq871qusDs_lo1/view?usp=sharing

Open &#8220;osm.org&#8221; in your browser, log in to your OSM profile and select &#8220;Edit&#8221; to enter Edit mode. Drag-n-drop the above GPX files into the OSM main window to overlay the GPS recorded trail. See Image 1 below.

https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=15/18.9519/73.5204

If you had already uploaded your recorded track through OSMAnd as a Public OSM trace then you can simply enable &#8220;OpenStreetMap GPS Traces&#8221; in the &#8220;Overlays&#8221; in the &#8220;Layers&#8221; icon. See Image 2 below.

GPS log overlaid on the osm.org Editor

Viewing Public Traces in the OSM editor

Mapping Trails

Zoom into the map until the &#8220;+ Zoom to Edit&#8221; message disappears. Click the &#8220;Line&#8221; feature to start drawing your trail in OSM. Similar to digitizing trails in QGIS (module 6D) keep clicking along the overlaid GPX file. Ensure to connect the start and end of your trail to existing OSM features (road, trail). Use the mouse scroller to zoom out/in while moving across your recorded trail. See Image 1 below.

You can map your GPS recording as multiple logical trail segments in OSM: place A to B, B to C, etc. Press ESC to complete one segment and select feature type as &#8220;Path&#8221; and give it a descriptive name. See Image 2 below.

Mapping a trail in OSM through the default osm.org editor

Marking a newly mapped trail as feature type &#8220;Path&#8221; in OSM

Uploading Trails

After completing all trail segments click the &#8220;Up arrow&#8221; top-right icon to upload the newly mapped trail sections to OSM. Provide a proper description and source (GPS recording) and click &#8220;Upload&#8221; to commit the changes. See Image 1 below.

After uploading the changes you can open your OSM Profile, &#8220;My Edits&#8221; to see the changes (only in the standard OSM basemap, other maps will take longer to reflect). See Image 2 below.

Uploading newly mapped trail sections to OSM

Viewing recently mapped OSM trail sections
