Important Notes

Bad weather (rain, snow) will introduce unexpected delays (several weeks overall)

Timeline is based on 20km / day mileage which is only realistic if you go lightweight (30L / 5kg) – lightweight gears + carry just sufficient food till next village

Sikkim – Survey maps of border regions are not available to public -to be planned

Nepal – I will reroute around 5 technical passes (no mountaineering gears) – Sherpani Col, West Col, Amphu Laptsa, Tashi Laptsa, Tilman Pass

Nepal/Bhutan – guides are mandatory for civilians – if applicable to you then please get in touch with authorized hiking agencies

Ladakh to Bhutan – route is accurate / GPS mapped

Arunachal – I planned a few alternate routes via Survey Maps. These are 1. not GPS accurate and 2. not sure if in use so might require rerouting

Unexpected snowfall might block certain passes – we can reroute as needed

Unta Dura is only way North of Nanda Devi (only open to military) – uncertain if this is non / technical pass. If technical, let me know, I can reroute South of the Nanda Devi NP

Buinder Khal connecting Valley of Flowers to Girthi Ganga valley is not mapped in OSM. Need Survey maps for border regions to plan this route

Monsoon

No impact of July monsoon in Ladakh, Spiti, Kinnaur

Mostly out of direct reach from the August monsoon in the high passes of Uttarakhand. Although fresh snowfall expected in upper regions. Rains hit during lower transit over Nepal border /Sharda river valley near Darchula. 

September monsoon mostly hits Kumaon. Team is out of monsoon reach in central Nepal

Snow

Snow cover analysis based on satellite data from ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 NDSI (Normalized Difference Snow Index) for year 2024

Aug 21 – Parang La and Pin Bhaba mostly snow free till 1-2 months after planned crossing. 

Aug 28 – no cloud free Satellite data available during Jul-Aug monsoon. First partial snow cover image for Lamkhaga, Kaliindhi, Unta Dura

Sep 17 – first clear satellite imagery on Sep 17 – significant snow cover on high passes of Uttarakhand (1.5 month later). Check with Uttarakhand hiking agencies (permits mandatory) on planned dates

Aug 30 – notable snow cover on West side of first high pass of Nepal – Krarang 5340m. Others are clear. No earlier clear Satellite imagery available due to monsoon cloud cover

Sep 19 – all clear for the high passes in East Nepal

Oct 1 – medium snow cover on high passes in central Nepal – Sangda La, Thorong La, Larkya La, Thilman’s pass

Oct 8 – medium snow cover on the passes in Eastern Nepal / EBC – Laptsa La, Sherpani Col, Lumba Samba

Oct 28 – similar snow cover on the high passes in Eastern Nepal. Touristic so mostly manageable

Nov 14 – very little snow on the high passes of Bhutan’s snowman trek (no changes in December due to Southern latitude of Bhutan)

Dec 11 – all clear on planned mid passes in Arunachal

Jan 5 – slightly more snow one month later in Arunachal but no snow close to planned traverse
