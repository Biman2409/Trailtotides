Trans Sikkim

 November 3, 2025

 Peter Van Geit

Tucked between Bhutan in the East (Tendu) with Nepal in the West (Sandakpu) we find the Himalayan state of Sikkim. To plan an East-West traverse across this region we partly use 1:50K Survey maps and partly Open Street Maps. Survey maps are not available for the immediate border regions and therefore we have limited coverage in the smaller state of Sikkim. The resulting 150km traverse takes us across 8 minor valleys and in-between passes.

Geography

Sikkim is basically one major valley formed by the Teesta and Rangit rivers draining the surrounding high ranges bordering Nepal (West), Tibet (North) and Bhutan (East). The traverse hops across 8 smaller valleys via in-between passes:
