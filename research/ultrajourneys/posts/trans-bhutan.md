Trans Bhutan

Continuing the Trans Arunachal route further West, the 500km Trans Bhutan traverse takes us from Tawang in Arunachal to Gangtok in Sikkim over 8 passes and many valleys via a mid range (sub 4000m) route. This section is part of the larger Trans Himalaya traverse from Arunachal to Kyrgyzstan.

Geography

There are 7 major river valleys flowing North to South. Showing the trans Bhutan traverse on an elevation map gives a clear understanding of the various valleys and in-between ranges being crossed while traversing East to West.

Food Supply

At an average mileage of 20km per day it would take around 25 days to cross over Bhutan. Assuming a food supply drop every week we would require 4-5 drop points:

Trashigang: 27.33274 91.54633

lingmethang: 27.26098 91.17137

Night Halts

Potential night halts every 20km can easily be identified using the km markers displayed by nakarte.me. Focus on villages, campsites or natural shelters nearby running water.
