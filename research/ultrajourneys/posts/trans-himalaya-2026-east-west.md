Important Notes

Going East to West has two limitations:

High passes in eastern Nepal are open late April / early May – start in Arunachal earliest Feb

Need to stay ahead of monsoon in Nepal (Jun) and Uttarakhand (July) – start in Arunachal latest Feb

Conclusion – East-West should start in Feb

Snowman trek in Bhutan is mostly not possible till April due to too much snow so we must go via the lower route

Winter Snow

Snow cover analysis based on satellite data from ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 NDSI (Normalized Difference Snow Index) for year 2024

27 Feb – Winter snow in the upper reaches / ridgelines of East Arunachal. Stay in the valleys / lower ridges .Limited snow on the two high passes crossed

12 Feb – Similar situation early Feb

11 Mar – Snow in upper reaches of Western Arunachal. Limited snow on the two high passes crossed

23 Mar – Snowman trek in Bhutan is covered in snow in March-April. Need to reroute via lower route which is snow free

11 Apr – Eastern Nepal still has lots of snow mid April. Preferred entry end April / May.

26 Apr – snow cover reduces significantly end April in Eastern Nepal. EBC still has significant snow.

May 3 – snow cover is reducing early around the EBC region

14 May – central Nepal (Annapurna, Manaslu) are opening up mid May

15 May – Western Nepal is mostly snow free mid May

Jul 9 – Unta Dhura, Kalindi Khal, Lamkhaga appear open

17 Jun – mid June Lamkhaga and Pin Bhaba are slowly opening up well on time for early July crossing
