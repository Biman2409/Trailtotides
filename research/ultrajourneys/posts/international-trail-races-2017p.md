International Trail Races 2017

Throughout 2017, I participated in various beautiful international trail races across Europe and Asia with varying distances of 100km to 100 miles.

Most races involve steep elevation gain, consist of very technical trails and (some have) alpine climate. All races require a mandatory self-supported running kit with basic gears.

Presentation Experiences & Learnings

Ultra Trail Panoramic

100 miler in Northwest Thailand organized by Teelakow

Grand Raid Reunion

Le Diagonales Des Fous, 100 miler on the volcanic island of Reunion off Madagascar

Tot Dret

130K run with 13.000 m of elevation gain in the Italian alps. Shorter brother of the Tor Des Geant

Grand Raid des Pyrenees

220km trail ultra in the beautiful Pyrenees South of France

Ultra Trail Koh Chang

100K run through the beautiful island of Koh Chang in Southeast Thailand
