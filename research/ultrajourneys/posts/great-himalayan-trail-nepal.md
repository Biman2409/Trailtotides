Great Himalayan Trail (Nepal)

 May 28, 2024

 Peter Van Geit

The Great Himalayan Trail runs East to West in Nepal extending further into India as the Great Western Himalayan Trail through Uttarakhand, Himachal, Ladakh and Kashmir. Recently the GHT was fully mapped in Open Street Maps.

This blog presents an overview of the 24 individual OSM sections using 1:180K scale topo maps across 49 valleys (Khola-s) and 52 mountain passes (Bhanjyang-s) as it traverses through the Koshi, Bagmati, Gondaki, Karnali and Sudurpashchim provinces.
